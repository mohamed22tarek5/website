Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verified answers about Mohamed Tarek Abdelhady's engineering expertise, embedded hardware services, and online simulation tools.

Who is Mohamed Tarek Abdelhady?

Mohamed Tarek Abdelhady is a Communication and Electronics Engineer based in Kafr el Sheikh, Egypt, specializing in embedded systems, custom PCB design, IoT device architectures, and full-stack web applications.

What embedded systems and hardware services are available?

End-to-end engineering services include custom firmware development for Arduino, ESP32, and STM32, sensor telemetry (I2C, SPI, UART), wireless connectivity (WiFi, BLE, LoRa), IoT dashboards, and rapid hardware prototyping.

What software and tools are used for custom PCB design?

Schematic capture, simulation, and multi-layer PCB layout are engineered in KiCad, EasyEDA, and Proteus to generate full production-ready Gerber RS-274X, Excellon drill, and BOM documentation for manufacturing.

Are the online engineering calculators and simulation tools free to use?

Yes. All 24 interactive calculators and engineering utilities—including Ohm's Law, Resistor Color Codes, Buck-Boost Design, and 555 Timer—operate 100% client-side in the browser, require no login, and are completely free.
