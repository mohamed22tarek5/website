Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verified answers about embedded engineering, PCB design capabilities, and hardware development workflows.

What embedded systems services does Mohamed Tarek provide?

Mohamed Tarek Abdelhady provides end-to-end embedded systems engineering, including custom firmware development for Arduino, ESP32, and STM32, sensor integration (I2C, SPI, UART), schematic capture, and PCB layout using KiCad and Proteus.

What software and tools are used for custom PCB design?

PCB design workflows utilize industry-standard CAD suites including KiCad, EasyEDA, and Proteus, generating production-ready Gerber RS-274X files, Excellon drill files, Bill of Materials (BOM), and pick-and-place files for PCB assembly.

Can custom Arduino and IoT prototypes be developed from concept to manufacturing?

Yes. Prototypes are engineered from initial architectural breadboard design through firmware development, wireless connectivity (WiFi, BLE, LoRa), cloud/dashboard telemetry integration, and final custom enclosure prototyping.
