Wire Size, Voltage Drop & Breaker Calculator

Dual-criteria conductor cross-section selection, voltage drop estimation, and coordinated circuit breaker sizing.

In Brief

Wire cross-sectional area is governed by two essential conditions: thermal current-carrying capacity (ampacity) according to standard installation tables, and voltage drop, which is recommended not to exceed 3% for lighting circuits and 5% for general feeder and branch power loads. This tool computes the smallest standard wire size satisfying both criteria and suggests the appropriate circuit breaker rating.

Engineering Notice: Results are provided for educational and preliminary estimation purposes. Electrical installations and real-world distribution boards must be engineered, verified, and stamped by a licensed electrical engineer in accordance with local and national electrical codes (e.g., IEC 60364 or NFPA 70 NEC).

Calculation Method & Governing Standards

Conductor sizing combines electrical thermodynamics with Ohm's law. In both IEC and NEC frameworks, a cable must satisfy both thermal steady-state heating and maximum permissible voltage drop limits:

1-Phase AC & DC Voltage Drop

ΔV = (2 × ρ × L × I × cosφ) / A

The factor 2 accounts for the loop length (live and neutral/return conductors). For DC, cosφ = 1.0.

3-Phase AC Balanced Voltage Drop

ΔV = (√3 × ρ × L × I × cosφ) / A

The factor √3 ≈ 1.732 reflects balanced line-to-line voltage in three-phase symmetric loads.

Conductor Resistivity (ρ)

ρ ≈ 0.0225 Ω·mm² / m

Resistivity of electrolytic copper adjusted for conductor operating temperature (~55°C–70°C) according to IEC standards.

Circuit Breaker Coordination

Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz

Load current Ib must not exceed breaker rating In, which in turn must not exceed cable ampacity Iz.

Practical Benchmark Example

3 kW Electric Water Heater on 230 V Supply (L = 25 m, ΔV ≤ 3%)

A single-phase resistive heating element of 3 kW (cosφ = 1.0) connected to a 230 V line at a one-way distance of 25 meters with a 3% voltage drop allowance:

1. Load Current (I) 3000 W / 230 V ≈ 13.04 A
2. Max Drop (ΔVmax) 230 V × 3% = 6.90 V
3. Required Min Area (2 × 0.0225 × 25 × 13.04) / 6.9 = 2.12 mm²
4. Selected Conductor 2.5 mm² (Ampacity = 27 A)
5. Coordinated Breaker 16 A MCB (13.04 ≤ 16 ≤ 27 A)
