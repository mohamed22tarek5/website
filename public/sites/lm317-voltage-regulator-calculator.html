LM317 Circuit Design Guidelines & Best Practices

Minimum Load Current

The LM317 requires a quiescent current between 3.5 mA and 10 mA (typically 5 mA) through OUT to maintain proper regulation. Setting R1 = 240 Ω guarantees 1.25V / 240Ω = 5.2 mA divider current, keeping it regulated even with no external load connected.

Dropout Voltage & Headroom

The LM317 is a standard linear regulator with a dropout voltage of 2.0V to 3.0V (worst-case under full 1.5A load over temperature). Always supply at least Vout + 3.0V at the input to prevent output ripple and loss of regulation.

Decoupling Capacitors

Place a 0.1 µF ceramic or 1 µF tantalum capacitor close to the IN pin to prevent high-frequency oscillations. Add a 1 µF to 10 µF capacitor on OUT for improved transient response and reduced ringing.

Reverse Protection Diodes

When Vout exceeds 25V or when using large output capacitors (>25 µF), add a 1N4002 diode from OUT (anode) to IN (cathode). This protects the LM317's internal pass transistor if the input power is abruptly shorted to ground.
