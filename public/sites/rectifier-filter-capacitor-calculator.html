Filter Capacitor Design Guide & Best Practices

Voltage Derating (+25% to +50%)

Always specify an electrolytic capacitor with a working voltage at least 25% higher than Vpeak (e.g., 25V rating for a 15.6V peak). AC mains voltage can fluctuate up to +10%, and exceeding the rated dielectric voltage drastically shortens electrolytic capacitor lifespan or causes venting.

Ripple Current & ESR Heating

In linear rectifiers, the capacitor charges in short, intense pulses. The RMS ripple current passing through the capacitor is typically 1.5× to 2.5× the DC load current. Select a high-ripple-current, 105°C rated capacitor to prevent internal overheating from ESR dissipation.

High-Frequency Noise Bypassing

Large electrolytic capacitors have significant parasitic internal inductance (ESL) at radio and audio frequencies. Always wire a 0.1 µF (100 nF) ceramic capacitor in parallel with the large reservoir capacitor to shunt high-frequency diode switching spikes to ground.

Linear Regulator Headroom

If feeding an LM7805, LM7812, or LM317 linear regulator after the filter, the minimum valley voltage (Vmin) must always remain higher than the regulated output plus the regulator dropout voltage (e.g. Vmin ≥ Vout + 2.5V) even at lowest mains line voltage.
