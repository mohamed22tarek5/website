# Ohm's Law Calculator

Calculate voltage (V), current (I), resistance (R), or power (P) using Ohm's Law formulas.

## Formulas

- **V = I × R** - Voltage equals current times resistance
- **I = V / R** - Current equals voltage divided by resistance
- **R = V / I** - Resistance equals voltage divided by current
- **P = V × I** - Power equals voltage times current

## Usage

Enter any two known values and the calculator will determine the remaining values.
