Design rules

1) Unloaded formula only holds with no load. 2) With load RL, use R₂′ = (R₂·RL)/(R₂+RL). 3) For low power pick R total 10k–100k; for stiff outputs pick ≤ 10k. 4) Resistor power rating ≥ 2× dissipated power.
