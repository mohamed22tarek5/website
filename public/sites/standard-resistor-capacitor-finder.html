Standard Component Values Guide

How EIA Logarithmic Series Work

E-series numbers are logarithmically spaced so that consecutive values have overlapping tolerance bands. E6 has 6 steps per decade ($\sqrt[6]{10} \approx 1.47$), E24 has 24 steps ($\sqrt[24]{10} \approx 1.10$), and E96 has 96 steps ($\sqrt[96]{10} \approx 1.024$).

Resistor vs. Capacitor Combination Rules

Resistors add in Series ($R_1 + R_2$) and divide in Parallel ($R_1 \parallel R_2$). Capacitors behave inversely: they add in Parallel ($C_1 + C_2$) and divide in Series ($C_1 \cdot C_2 / (C_1 + C_2)$).

Store Availability & Cost

E12 and E24 values are standard commodity stock and cost pennies. E96 1% metal-film resistors are widely available in surface-mount (SMD 0805, 0603) and 1/4W through-hole, while E192 (0.1%–0.5%) are specialty high-cost parts.

Tolerances & Temperature Drift

Picking an exact E96 resistor does not eliminate drift. Carbon resistors can drift 5% over temperature, while 1% metal-film resistors have temperature coefficients typically around ±50 to ±100 ppm/°C.
