In Brief

Battery storage capacity is determined by dividing total required energy (Daily Consumption × Days of Autonomy) by the System Voltage, Depth of Discharge (DoD), and Inverter Efficiency. Then, the number of batteries in series (Ns) is determined by dividing system voltage by individual battery voltage, while parallel branches (Np) are determined by dividing required capacity by individual battery Ah rating.

Engineering Notice: Results are provided for educational and preliminary estimation purposes. High-current battery banks and high-voltage DC installations must be designed, fused, and installed by a certified electrical engineer in accordance with local national safety codes.

Engineering Calculation Methodology

Solar battery storage sizing guarantees that energy stored during sunlight hours reliably sustains connected loads across autonomy periods without exceeding the safe discharge boundary of the battery chemistry:

1. Required Battery Capacity (Ah)

Ah = (E × Days) / (η × V × DoD)

Where E is daily consumption (Wh), Days is autonomy, η is inverter efficiency, V is system voltage, and DoD is depth of discharge.

2. Batteries in Series (Ns)

Ns = Vsystem / Vbattery

Connecting batteries in series adds voltages together while maintaining the same ampere-hour (Ah) capacity.

3. Parallel Branches (Np)

Np = ⌈ Ahreq / Ahbattery ⌉

Connecting strings in parallel adds capacity (Ah) together while keeping the nominal system voltage identical.

4. Total Battery Count (Ntotal)

Ntotal = Ns × Np

Total physical units required to construct the battery bank matrix.

Best Installation & Wiring Practices:

  • Parallel String Limit: It is strongly recommended not to exceed 3 or 4 parallel strings. Excess parallel branches create unequal internal resistances, causing circulating currents and prematurely degrading weaker strings.
  • High Capacity Alternative: If more capacity is needed, select higher capacity individual cells (e.g., 200 Ah or 300 Ah units) or elevate system DC bus voltage to 48 V.
  • Cross-Corner Connection: Connect the main inverter Positive (+) lead to the first string, and the main Negative (-) lead to the opposite diagonal corner of the last string to equalize charge distribution across all cells.
